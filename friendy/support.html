Frequently asked questions

How do I make friends on Friendy?

Open the Discover tab to see people who share your interests — matching interests are highlighted in pink. Tap Add friend to connect and start chatting, or Skip to pass (you can reshow skipped people anytime).

How do I block or report someone?

Tap the ⋯ button on any profile card or at the top of any chat. Blocking hides the person everywhere instantly and they are not notified. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours and violating users are removed.

How do I edit my profile?

Go to Profile → Edit profile to change your avatar, name, or bio at any time.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Profile → Delete my account & data. This permanently erases your profile, friends, and all messages from your device. It cannot be undone.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored locally on your own device — Friendy has no server accounts, no ads, and no trackers. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What are the age requirements?

Friendy is for people 13 and older. Users under 18 should have a parent or guardian's permission.

Can I install Friendy on my home screen?

Yes! Open Friendy in Safari (iPhone/iPad) and tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It installs like a regular app with its own icon and works offline.
