Community Guidelines

Friendy works because everyone here is kind. Here's the deal.

The Friendy promise

Friendy is a place to make real friends — not to date, sell, troll, or intimidate. Every person you see here agreed to these rules, and we enforce them. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours, and rule-breaking content and accounts are removed.

💛
Be kind, always. Talk to people the way you'd want a good friend to talk to you. Disagreeing is fine; being cruel is not.
🚫
No harassment or hate. Bullying, threats, hate speech, and discrimination of any kind get you removed. Zero tolerance, no second chances for serious abuse.
🔞
Keep it friendly, not explicit. Friendy is for friendship. Sexual content, graphic violence, and shock content don't belong here.
🎭
Be yourself. Use your real first name and an honest bio. Impersonating others or lying about your age breaks trust — and the rules.
💸
No spam or scams. Don't advertise, solicit money, push crypto schemes, or send the same message to everyone.
🔒
Protect privacy. Never share someone else's personal information, and be careful with your own. Don't ask new friends for addresses, financial details, or passwords.
🧒
Look out for younger users. Friendy is 13+. If you're an adult, be a good example. Suspected underage accounts are removed.

Your safety toolkit

  • Block — instantly hides a person everywhere in the app. They are not notified.
  • Report — tap ⋯ on any profile or chat to report. Every report is reviewed within 24 hours and violating content or users are removed.
  • Message filtering — abusive language is automatically blocked from being sent.
  • Delete your data — erase your entire account anytime from Profile.

What happens when rules are broken

Depending on severity: content removal, a warning, temporary suspension, or permanent removal. Serious violations (threats, hate speech, targeting minors) result in immediate permanent removal and, where appropriate, reports to authorities.
