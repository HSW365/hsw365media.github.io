Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 20, 2026 · Friendy v1.0

💛 The short version: Friendy stores your profile, friends, and messages only on your device. We don't run accounts on a server, we don't sell data, and we don't show ads. Delete the app (or use “Delete my account & data”) and everything is gone.

1. Who we are

Friendy (“we”, “us”) is a friend-making app operated by QUEENEE.io. Questions about this policy can be sent to hoodstarent365@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Friendy is a local-first app. The information you enter is stored in your device's local storage and never transmitted to our servers:

  • Profile data — your first name, avatar, short bio, and chosen interests.
  • Activity data — friends you've added, people you've skipped or blocked, and your messages.
  • Settings — preferences such as your notification toggle.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, health data, or advertising identifiers. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. How your information is used

Your information is used solely to make the app work: showing your profile to you, matching interests to suggested friends, and displaying your conversations. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Because your data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to share.

5. Data retention & deletion

Data remains on your device until you remove it. You can erase everything at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete my account & data — permanently erases your profile, friends, and messages.
  • Or simply uninstall the app / clear the site's browsing data.

6. Children's privacy

Friendy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users aged 13–17 should use Friendy with a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 is using Friendy, contact us at hoodstarent365@gmail.com.

7. Security

Friendy is served over HTTPS and stores data using your device's standard app storage protections. Because no data is transmitted to servers, the risk surface is limited to your own device — we recommend using a device passcode.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since all Friendy data lives on your device and under your control, you can exercise these rights directly through the app at any time. For anything else, email us.

9. Changes to this policy

If we materially change how Friendy handles data (for example, by adding online accounts in a future version), we will update this policy, change the effective date above, and notify you in the app before any new collection begins.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: hoodstarent365@gmail.com
